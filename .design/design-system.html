Design System

01 — Philosophy

Warmth that still counts every item

Wafina sits between two feelings that usually don't share a screen: the warmth of giving, and the precision of logistics. A donor needs to feel good about a bag of winter clothes; an institution needs to know exactly what's arriving and when. The system leans warm in color and voice, and leans precise in data — timestamps, quantities and IDs are always set in a monospace face so they line up and read as facts, not decoration.

Two brand hues carry this: Bougainvillea, a deep magenta named for the flowering vine common across Luanda's walls and verandas, used for the moments that need energy — primary actions, the brand mark, progress. Palm, a deep teal-green, carries trust and secondary structure — links, secondary actions, confirmation. Neither is borrowed from the national flag; both are drawn from the everyday landscape instead of the symbolism, which is what keeps the palette feeling like a place rather than a slogan.

03 — Typography

Fraunces, Work Sans & IBM Plex Mono

Fraunces (a warm, soft-curved serif) carries headings and the rare editorial moment — it reads generous rather than corporate. Work Sans, a humanist grotesque with excellent Portuguese diacritic support, carries every interface and body text. IBM Plex Mono is reserved for anything that needs to line up in a column: IDs, timestamps, quantities.

06 — Elevation & radius

Soft shadows in light, borders in dark

Dark surfaces read elevation through a lighter fill and a hairline border rather than a shadow, which tends to muddy on dark backgrounds. Radius scales with a component's size: small controls stay tight, cards and sheets get generous corners.

Top app bar & bottom navigation

17 — Responsive rules

Mobile-first, web second

Every component is authored for a 375px viewport first, then progressively enhanced. Below 640px: single column, bottom nav, sheets instead of modals, 16px outer padding. 640–1024px: two-column forms, modals replace sheets. Above 1024px: persistent left rail instead of bottom nav, 12-column grid, 24px gutters, content capped at 1280px.

18 — Accessibility

Baseline, not aspiration

Contrast

AA minimum — 4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text and UI components. Every swatch above is checked against its typical text pairing.

Touch targets

44×44pt minimum on mobile, 8px minimum gap between adjacent targets.

Focus

Every interactive element gets a visible 2px accent outline on keyboard focus — try tabbing through the buttons above.

Motion

prefers-reduced-motion disables shimmer and spinner animation site-wide.

Structure

Semantic landmarks and ARIA roles over div soup; every icon-only button carries an aria-label.

Type scaling

Rem-based type respects the user's OS/browser font-size setting; nothing is locked in px.

19 — Naming conventions

One name, two platforms

Components share a name and a props contract across web and mobile even though the underlying element differs — a designer or reviewer should never have to translate "Button" into "Pressable" in conversation.
