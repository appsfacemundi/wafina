  as-is. If you edit any field, re-check length before saving (Play/Apple both reject over-limit
  text at save time, so this isn't a silent risk).
- **Spanish/French/Chinese — drafted 2026-08-12, still needs native/professional review before
  use.** Full drafts now exist for both apps (see the "Other languages" subsection under each app),
  covering short description, subtitle, promotional text, keywords, full description, and What's
  New — all character counts measured and within limit. **These are machine-assisted drafts, not
  verified for idiomatic quality** — I can reason about correctness and completeness, but I can't
  personally vouch for how natural these read to a native speaker the way I can for PT/EN. Treat
  them as a strong starting point for a translator to edit, not as paste-ready final copy. Not
  blocking RC1 either way — Play/Apple both support adding listing languages after the initial
  submission, so this can also wait until after launch if you'd rather not review translations
  right now.
- Both apps' full descriptions deliberately avoid claiming anything not verifiable in the code:
  no "instant," no unverified statistics, no payment/donation-processing language beyond what
  `apps/web/src/app/terms` already states.

  decided (`support@zuinder.com`; Privacy Policy at the deployed `/privacy` page on Donor Web;
